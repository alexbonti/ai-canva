# Welcome to the AI Canva Project — Intro Message

> Post this to students to kick off the AI Canva project.

---

**📣 Welcome to the AI Canva Project**

Hi everyone,

Over the next **3 sprints (8 weeks)**, you'll be working on **AI Canva** — a real, working AI-powered whiteboard where you build visual "pipelines" of boxes (Idea → Research → PRD → Code) and let AI content flow from one box to the next.

**Your job:** extend AI Canva by building your own **AI boxes** — and, just as importantly, research and justify *why* your boxes exist. The build is AI-assisted and achievable; the real work is the thinking behind it.

**Everything you need is in the project repo** — start here:

1. 📖 **What is AI Canva?** — read `docs/course/01_what_is_ai_canva.md` first to understand the tool
2. 📋 **Your project brief** — read the one assigned to you:
   - **Individual:** `docs/course/02_student_brief_one_box.md`
   - **Cybersecurity & Compliance:** `docs/course/03_cyber_group_brief.md`
   - **UX for Telstra Health:** `docs/course/04_ux_health_group_brief.md`
   - **Telstra Innovation Lab:** `docs/course/06_telstra_innovation_brief.md`
   - **NBN Collaboration:** `docs/course/07_nbn_collab_brief.md`
3. 🛠️ **How to build a box** — `docs/course/05_how_to_build_a_box.md` walks you through your first box step-by-step, including the git/branching rules

> 💡 Each guide also has a print-friendly **HTML version** in the same folder.

**Your first tasks (Sprint 1):**

1. **Read** the "What is AI Canva?" guide
2. **Read your brief** and pick your box / lock your team roles
3. **Choose your research question** and find 3 sources
4. **Clone the repo** and get it running (`npm run dev`)
5. **Register your first box** in `types.ts`

Remember: **research counts for a big part of your grade.** Build it, but more importantly, understand *why* you built it that way.

Any questions, just ask. Let's get going! 🚀
